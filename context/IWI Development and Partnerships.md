# IWI Development and Partnerships

## BLUF

At the Irregular Warfare Initiative, I helped build the development and partnerships function for a mission-driven 501(c)(3), including institutional sponsorship, corporate partnership growth, donor engagement, and sponsor alignment.

## Role

Director of Development and Partnerships.

Previously used Chief Development Officer title.

## Organization Context

The Irregular Warfare Initiative is a 501(c)(3) focused on irregular warfare analysis, education, and public discourse.

Key audience metrics:

* Podcast audience of approximately 10,000 downloads per episode
* Newsletter reach of approximately 10,000
* Online publication platform

## Key Accomplishments

### Institutional Sponsorship Program

Built and launched an institutional sponsorship program in 2025.

Secured the first multi-year institutional sponsor for 75,000 dollars over three years.

### Sponsorship Growth

Grew corporate sponsors by ten between CY24 and CY25.

Helped drive a 50 percent net increase in top-line budget.

### Public Fundraising

Led or supported a public fundraising campaign that produced a 10 times year-over-year increase in donations from CY24 to CY25.

### Sponsorship Structure

Created sponsorship prospectus and tiered sponsorship offerings, including concepts for:

* Podcast season sponsorship
* Podcast episode sponsorship
* Newsletter sponsorship
* Student fellowship sponsorship
* Research fellowship sponsorship
* Event sponsorship

### Sponsor Alignment

Drafted policy and language to preserve sponsor alignment and IWI’s apolitical stance.

### Events and Partnerships

Supported or developed concepts for events including:

* South America in Competition conference
* SOAA co-hosted event concepts
* Critical minerals and rare earth elements event themes
* Supply chain, illicit networks, SOF, IC, and public/private partnership themes
