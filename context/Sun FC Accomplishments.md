# Sun FC Accomplishments

## BLUF

At Tampa Bay Sun FC, I helped a startup professional women’s soccer club build systems, structure, and cross-functional alignment across ticketing, marketing, communications, partnerships, operations, and community engagement. This role gave me direct proof that my Special Forces and SOCOM background translates into sports business.

## Stronger Summary After Reviewing Source Documents

My Sun FC work produced a complete Marketing & Communications operating system, including brand strategy, communications guidance, campaign governance, HubSpot and UTM standards, SeatGeek attribution logic, matchday execution processes, league compliance workflows, media operations, and onboarding materials.

This work is best framed as sports business infrastructure. It connected brand, revenue, fan growth, matchday experience, and operational discipline.

## Role

Chief of Staff through the U.S. Special Operations Command Warrior Care Program fellowship.

Also served in an acting Director of Marketing and Communications capacity during the fellowship period.

## Core Value Provided

I helped turn a fast-moving startup sports environment into a more aligned, disciplined, and structured organization by building operating systems, planning cadences, campaign processes, reporting mechanisms, and cross-department synchronization.

## Key Accomplishments

### Cross-Functional Alignment

Helped align:

* Ticketing
* Marketing
* Communications
* Partnerships
* Operations
* Community engagement
* Matchday execution

This work helped ensure that departments were operating from the same picture of the audience, the opportunity, and the business objective.

### Brand Strategy

Created and refined brand strategy, including:

* Club vision
* Values
* Core audiences
* Fan-facing positioning
* Internal operating standard
* Messaging around Ybor, Tampa Bay, women’s sports, and community

Relevant brand language:

* Saturday nights in Ybor equal Sun soccer
* Women’s soccer lives in Ybor
* We play for each other, for Ybor, and for Tampa Bay
* Rooted in Ybor City and powered by its energy, culture, and people

### Operating Systems and SOPs

Built or contributed to:

* Marketing and communications playbook
* Matchday marketing and communications SOP
* Campaign planning SOP
* Executive reporting formats
* Monthly reporting to Christina Unkel and ownership
* Internal planning cadences
* Department synchronization mechanisms

### Fan Growth and Revenue Alignment

Worked to connect marketing and ticketing activity to measurable fan growth and revenue outcomes.

Relevant reported results:

* 20 percent increase in average match attendance
* 3 times increase in vendor activations
* 10 percent increase in per-capita revenue
* Average attendance increased from approximately 1,200 in 2025 toward 1,500 to 2,000 range
* Long-term target of 3,000 average attendance next season
* Stadium capacity target of 5,000

### CRM, Data, and Fan Journey

Helped structure CRM and fan data capture across:

* HubSpot
* SeatGeek links
* UTMs
* QR codes
* Landing pages
* Social campaigns
* Community events
* Meta Ads
* Matchday activations

Built a clearer distinction between:

* High-intent fans routed through HubSpot capture before SeatGeek
* Lower-intent fans routed directly to SeatGeek through trackable links

Explored SeatGeek Ringside API to sync purchases into HubSpot.

### Stadium and Operations Context

Worked in the context of Suncoast Credit Union Field in Ybor, with 5,000-seat capacity target and co-use arrangement with Blake High School.

Relevant stadium issues:

* Suncoast Credit Union naming rights
* Sun branding
* Blake High School branding
* Avoiding painted logos on turf due to repainting costs
* Exploring portable turf logo options
* 9 million dollar capital investment to bring stadium toward league standards

## Championship Context

Tampa Bay Sun FC won the inaugural USL Super League championship. The club beat Fort Lauderdale United and celebrated with a boat parade. The championship created an opportunity to update the crest with a star and build a stronger defending champion narrative.
