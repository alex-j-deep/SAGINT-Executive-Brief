# Sun FC Playbook

## BLUF

The Sun FC Playbook is the clearest proof point that my Special Forces and SOCOM background translated directly into sports business. At Tampa Bay Sun FC, I helped build a practical operating system for a startup professional women’s soccer club by connecting vision, revenue, operations, marketing, communications, fan growth, matchday execution, and measurement.

This was not just marketing support. It was strategy implementation, cross-functional alignment, campaign governance, revenue attribution, matchday synchronization, and brand-building discipline.

## Core Narrative

Tampa Bay Sun FC had a clear ambition: to become a best-in-class professional women’s soccer club connected to the community and the voice for women’s sports in Tampa Bay. My work helped turn that aspiration into usable systems, including a brand strategy, communications guide, campaign management SOP, marketing and communications playbook, matchday SOP, and onboarding materials.

The common thread across the work was simple: turn vision into execution.

## Strategic Framework

The Sun strategy was organized around three core pillars:

### Revenue

Endstate: The Sun become one of the top revenue-generating clubs in the USL Super League, with a sellout-driven matchday environment and a corporate partner portfolio that reflects leading brands in Tampa Bay and women’s sports nationally.

Measures included:

* 90%+ average stadium capacity
* Multi-year anchor corporate partners
* Matchday revenue per capita among league leaders
* Sponsorship inventory largely sold before season kickoff
* Corporate partners activating at matches and in the community

### Operations

Endstate: The Sun deliver one of the best matchday experiences in women’s soccer, with operational standards that rival top professional clubs.

Measures included:

* Smooth, safe, energetic matchdays
* Strong stadium identity in Ybor
* Professionalized internal processes
* Clear decision structures
* Players and staff proud to represent the organization

### Marketing & Communications

Endstate: The Sun become the recognized voice of women’s sports in Tampa Bay, with a powerful brand rooted in Ybor City and a growing national profile.

Measures included:

* Strong media coverage across Tampa Bay
* Large and engaged digital following
* Players recognized as community leaders and role models
* Brand identity associated with Ybor City and women’s sports

## Campaign Management System

The Marketing Campaign Management SOP created a standardized process for how the Marketing Department uses HubSpot to plan, execute, and measure campaigns.

### Purpose

* Ensure all marketing efforts are tied to HubSpot campaigns
* Track traffic sources using UTM parameters
* Attribute ticket sales to specific marketing initiatives
* Improve coordination between Marketing and Ticketing
* Provide leadership with accurate campaign performance insights

### Scope

Applied to both digital and grassroots marketing, including:

* Organic social media
* Paid social media
* Email marketing
* Digital advertising
* Website promotions
* Influencer and partner promotions
* Community activations
* Street team campaigns
* Partner promotions
* Ticket sales booths
* In-stadium activations
* Sweepstakes and giveaways

### Campaign Structure

No marketing effort should launch without a HubSpot campaign created first.

Each campaign should include:

* Campaign tracking URLs
* UTM parameters
* Associated landing pages
* Contact lists
* Revenue attribution
* Campaign reporting metrics

### Campaign Flows

#### High-Intent Lead Capture Campaigns

Used for database growth and lead capture.

Examples:

* Community activations
* Youth soccer partnerships
* Street team events
* Enter-to-win promotions
* Sweepstakes
* Newsletter signups

Flow:

1. Fan scans QR code or clicks campaign link
2. Link directs user to HubSpot landing page
3. Fan submits name and email
4. HubSpot creates a contact
5. User is redirected to SeatGeek or confirmation page

#### Direct Ticket Purchase Campaigns

Used for immediate ticket sales.

Examples:

* Social media posts
* Paid advertising
* Email promotions
* Ticketing promotions
* Ticket booths
* Match week promotions

Flow:

1. Fan scans QR code or clicks link
2. Link directs user directly to SeatGeek
3. URL includes campaign UTMs
4. SeatGeek tracks ticket purchases tied to those UTMs

## Revenue Attribution

The SOP created a phased approach for ticket revenue attribution.

### Phase 1: Manual Attribution

Marketing maintains UTMs, Ticketing pulls SeatGeek reports, purchaser information is uploaded into HubSpot, and revenue is associated with the relevant campaign.

### Phase 2: Amplify Platform Reporting

Marketing begins pulling SeatGeek reports independently to speed campaign attribution and reporting.

### Phase 3: Ringside API Integration

SeatGeek Ringside API integration with HubSpot enables automatic contact creation, campaign attribution, deal creation, real-time ticket purchase reporting, and influenced contact tracking.

## Reporting Model

Marketing monthly reports should summarize:

* Campaign revenue
* Tickets sold by campaign
* Influenced contacts
* Conversion rate
* Cost per ticket sold
* Fan database growth

This is an important business analytics proof point for future roles.

## Matchday SOP

The Marketing & Communications Matchday SOP created a standardized H-hour framework for executing all Marketing and Communications activities on matchday.

### Purpose

* Coordinate execution across Digital, Grassroots, and Communications
* Deliver a best-in-class fan experience from arrival through egress
* Meet league-mandated content and media requirements
* Maximize fan engagement, content capture, and brand visibility
* Maintain consistency and accountability

### Scope

Included:

* Social media
* Email marketing
* Content capture
* Scoreplay and league content delivery
* Activation lawn setup
* Ingress and egress engagement
* Giveaways
* Fan experience elements
* Media operations
* Press releases
* Match reports
* Press conference coordination

### H-Hour Framework

All activities were synchronized relative to kickoff.

Key phases:

* H-48: match preparation
* H-3: arrival and setup
* H-2: pre-match content and media operations
* H-1: final pre-match execution
* H-45 to H-30: fan ingress
* H: kickoff and live match execution
* Halftime: reset and peak engagement
* H+75: egress and media closeout preparation
* H+90: final whistle and immediate post-match outputs
* H+1.5 to H+2: press operations and digital fulfillment
* Post-match: breakdown and quick debrief

## League Compliance

The matchday SOP captured mandatory league requirements.

### Scoreplay

* Halftime: 20 photos
* Post-match: 10 photos within 15 minutes
* 24 hours: 40 additional photos
* Penalty: $500 fine for non-compliance

### Social Media

Required content included:

* Matchday graphic
* Starting XI
* Goal posts
* Substitutions
* Halftime graphics
* Full-time graphics
* Penalty: $150 per missing post

## Away Match Contingencies

The SOP included addendums for:

* Away matches with staff traveling
* Away matches with no traveling staff

This is a strong example of disciplined operational planning. The plan accounted for reduced staffing, limited original content capture, reliance on host club infrastructure, broadcast feeds, league assets, and remote execution.
