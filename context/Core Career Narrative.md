# Core Career Narrative

## Short Version

I am a strategy and operations leader transitioning from Army Special Forces into sports business. My career has focused on turning vision into execution, aligning complex organizations, building high-performing teams, and creating operating systems that help leaders move faster with more clarity.

## Expanded Version

I spent 20 years in Army Special Forces, including senior strategy work at U.S. Special Operations Command, where I helped translate senior leader vision into strategy, stakeholder alignment, and implementation. During my transition, I have applied that same skill set in professional sports with Tampa Bay Sun FC, helping align ticketing, marketing, communications, partnerships, operations, and community engagement around fan growth and revenue priorities. At Tampa Bay Sun FC, I did not simply support marketing. I built the operating systems that connected brand, revenue, fan growth, campaign measurement, communications, and matchday execution inside a startup professional sports club. I also lead development and partnerships for the Irregular Warfare Initiative, a 501(c)(3), where I built sponsorship structures, secured institutional support, and helped grow fundraising and partnerships.

## What I Am Really Selling

I help organizations move from ambition to execution.

That includes:

* Clarifying the vision
* Building the strategy
* Aligning the stakeholders
* Creating the operating rhythm
* Measuring progress
* Communicating clearly
* Keeping teams moving in the same direction

## Best Fit Environments

* Ambitious organizations with growth opportunities
* Executive leaders who need a trusted integrator
* Sports organizations trying to professionalize systems
* Mission-driven organizations with public trust responsibilities
* Teams that need cross-functional alignment
* Organizations navigating change, growth, or transformation

## Language to Reuse

I turn vision into operating systems.

I help senior leaders convert intent into action.

I build alignment across teams that need to move together but often operate in silos.

I bring a rare combination of elite team leadership, enterprise strategy, nonprofit development, and direct sports business execution.

I am not trying to trade on my military background. I am translating it into a sports business context where structure, culture, execution, and alignment matter.
