That is the kind of work I have done throughout my career.

## Common Interview Answers

## Tell Me About Yourself

I recently retired as an Army Special Forces officer after 20 years of service, with my later career focused heavily on strategy, leadership, and organizational alignment at U.S. Special Operations Command. During my transition, I have been very intentional about moving into sports business. I joined Tampa Bay Sun FC through a fellowship and quickly found that the same skills translated directly: turning vision into strategy, aligning departments, building operating systems, and helping teams execute. I also lead development and partnerships for the Irregular Warfare Initiative, a 501(c)(3), where I built sponsorship and fundraising systems. So the throughline is that I help organizations turn ambition into disciplined execution.

## Why Sports?

Sports organizations are mission-driven, community-facing, emotionally resonant, and operationally complex. That combination is very familiar to me. What I saw at the Sun confirmed that my background translates directly. The problems are different, but the leadership challenge is similar: align people, clarify priorities, build systems, and execute in a way that earns trust.

## Why Are You Competitive Despite a Nontraditional Background?

Because many of the hardest problems in sports are not narrow technical problems. They are alignment, leadership, strategy, communication, growth, and execution problems. I bring a different background, but I have already applied it inside a professional sports organization and created tangible systems that helped the club operate more effectively.

## How to Explain the Sun Fellowship

The Sun role was part of the USSOCOM Warrior Care Program fellowship, but it became a real operating role very quickly. I served as Chief of Staff and acted as Director of Marketing and Communications, helping build the systems behind brand strategy, campaign planning, CRM tracking, matchday execution, communications, and executive reporting.
