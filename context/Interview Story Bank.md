# Interview Story Bank

## Story 1: Sun FC Operating System

### Situation

Startup professional sports club needed more structure across marketing, communications, ticketing, partnerships, operations, and matchday execution.

### Action

Built playbook, campaign SOP, matchday SOP, communications guide, brand strategy, and HubSpot / SeatGeek tracking logic.

### Result

Created a repeatable operating system connecting brand, revenue, fan growth, and execution.

### Use For

Bucs, USF, Hornets, Fractional Chief of Staff, sports strategy roles.
