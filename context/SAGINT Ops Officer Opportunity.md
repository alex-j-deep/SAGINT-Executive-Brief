## Organization Context

SAGINT is a founder-led national security technology company building traceability and digital asset infrastructure for critical minerals and other strategically important supply chains.

Jacob frames SAGINT as building an **economic warfare operating system**.

The company’s broader ambition is to help the United States and its allies compete at the market infrastructure level by creating trusted traceability systems that make strategic materials compliant, bankable, insurable, and enforceable.

## Company Thesis

SAGINT’s thesis is that the United States and its allies are not competing effectively against China at the level of **market infrastructure**.

The company is focused first on critical minerals, especially rare earths and strategically important inputs such as magnets, tantalum, and tungsten.

Key thesis points:

- China controls and manipulates key parts of the critical minerals market.
- The United States is focused too much on supply chains and not enough on market infrastructure.
- Current traceability systems are often top-down, expensive, and hard to scale.
- Financial institutions need trusted data before they can underwrite products tied to critical minerals.
- Government needs transaction level visibility to enforce compliance, sanctions, and supply chain restrictions.
- A neutral, non-custodial traceability layer could make critical minerals compliant, bankable, insurable, and enforceable.
- Compliance must become easier and cheaper than evasion.

## Product and Platform

SAGINT is building a non-custodial traceability and digital asset infrastructure platform.

The platform is intended to:

- Receive data from independent validators
- Create controllable electronic records under UCC Chapter 12
- Support trade finance, insurance, delivery insurance, commodity linked notes, and possibly ETFs
- Make assets bankable and insurable
- Provide a trusted through-line for banks, insurers, assurance firms, OEMs, and government
- Enable economic warfare actions through enforceable digital records
- Serve as trusted infrastructure, similar to Stripe for supply chains

SAGINT does not mine, refine, validate, finance, or physically touch the assets. Instead, it creates the infrastructure that receives validated data and makes the underlying asset more usable for finance, insurance, compliance, and enforcement.
