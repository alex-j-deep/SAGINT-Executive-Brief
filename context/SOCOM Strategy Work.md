# SOCOM Strategy Work

## BLUF

At U.S. Special Operations Command, I operated at the senior strategy level inside a complex global enterprise. My core value was helping translate senior leader vision into strategy, implementation frameworks, stakeholder alignment, and decision support.

## Role Context

Served as Chief / Deputy Chief of Strategy and Analysis at U.S. Special Operations Command.

Led a strategy and analysis organization of approximately 30 people and contributed to strategic planning in a 12 billion dollar enterprise context.

## Core Work

### Vision to Strategy

Helped translate GEN Bryan Fenton’s vision into an actionable strategy that respected the legacy of Special Operations while preparing the enterprise for future challenges.

### Enterprise Alignment

Worked across internal and external stakeholders to align priorities, language, planning, and implementation.

### Campaign Planning

Helped ensure strategy became executable through campaign planning, decision frameworks, operating rhythms, and follow-through mechanisms.

### Strategic Communication

Served as a trusted representative for senior leader priorities and helped communicate strategy to internal and external audiences.

### Decision Support

Supported senior leader decision making through analysis, frameworks, prioritization, and structured thinking.

## Key Themes

* Turn vision into strategy
* Turn strategy into execution
* Respect legacy while driving change
* Align stakeholders across a complex enterprise
* Build operating systems around leader intent
* Communicate clearly to internal and external audiences
* Create disciplined processes for implementation

## Sports Translation

This experience translates directly into sports business because sports organizations also require:

* Clear vision

## How to Explain This in Sports Language

I helped senior leaders move from vision to executable strategy across a complex enterprise. That same skill applies to sports organizations trying to align ticketing, marketing, partnerships, operations, analytics, community impact, and executive priorities around measurable growth.
